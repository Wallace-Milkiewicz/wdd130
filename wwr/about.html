White Water Rafting

We are a family-owned rafting company that has guided thousands of people down wild rivers. Our certified guides make every trip safe, fun, and unforgettable, whether it is your first time on the water or your fiftieth.

History

Our story started in 1985, when two friends bought a single raft and began taking neighbors down the river on weekends. Word spread quickly, and within a few years they were running daily trips through the canyon.

Today we operate a fleet of modern rafts and a team of experienced guides. We still hold on to the same values from our first day: respect for the river, safety first, and a great time for every guest.
